How to check your sizes:

You can check your sizes using either the coin method, or the tape method listed below.

Coin Method:

  1. Lay your hands on a flat surface and relax your fingers, keeping them straight.
  2. Place a coin next to the widest part of your natural nail, usually your index or middle finger. If you're taking a picture of only one finger, place the coin directly next to it.
  3. Hold your camera directly above the hand. Do not take pictures from an angle, and please make sure your nails and coin are visible.
  4. Take a separate photo of your thumb, following the above process.
  5. Repeat this process for both hands.

Once done, send me all of the images for both hands.

Tape Method:

  1. Prep your nails by removing any old polish, extensions or previous press-ons.
  2. Apply clear tape, or masking tape, horizontally across the widest part of your natural nail. Press the tape down, allowing it to crease into the edges of your nails sidewalls.
  3. Mark both of the edges of your nail using a pen.
  4. Peel the tape off and stick it onto a piece of paper. Use a ruler to measure the distance between the two lines in millimeters.
  5. Repeat this process for both hands.

Once done, send me all of the sizes in millimeters for both hands. If possible, please indicate which hand and finger the sizes are for!
